A Million Worlds

November 2026

Software is experiencing unprecedented acceleration. The loop between writing code, testing it, and learning from failure keeps getting shorter. Excellent coding harnesses build environments to run and test software, ad infinitum.

What about the physical world?

You can’t copy a warehouse a million times. You can’t crash a drone to check every commit, or wait for rain to test a perception change. Testing a thousand robots means having a thousand robots.

Simulation is the solution. Yet teams are stuck wrangling infrastructure, manually building environments, and digging through failures. Maintenance kills progress.

The bottleneck isn't ideas, and increasingly it isn't models.

Antioch is the simulation infrastructure to scale physical AI. High-fidelity digital twins. Real-world sensors, accurately modeled. Physically accurate worlds. Millions of scenarios. Failures reconstructed to expand domains.

What environments do you want to support? How do you want your system to behave? What new capabilities do you want to train? Antioch brings simulation, evaluation, and training into one continuous workflow.
